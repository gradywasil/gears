## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Authored-for-this-product. The steel ramp encodes physics (size reads as
speed); the ghost verdict rings visualize the placement solver's actual decision — no
generic app has them. DESIGN.md tokens are traceable line-by-line into styles.css. The one
category-interchangeable surface: the Saves panel (a generic 6-row CRUD grid).

**Deterministic scan:** 12 advisory findings (exit 2). design-system-color ×8
(spriteCache stroke/hub hexes; two `#fff` in CSS), design-system-font-size ×4. In-page
overlay run confirmed 9 anti-patterns: undersized-ui-text ×8 (tray labels 9.92px),
flat-type-hierarchy, cream-palette.

**False positives (assessed):** `#fff` shorthands = documented tokens (exact-string miss);
spriteCache strokes/hubs = intentional-but-undocumented ramp members; cream-palette =
Bench Paper is the sanctioned world ground; font-size "ramp" findings cite a ramp that
DESIGN.md deliberately doesn't define. The 9.92px tray labels are NOT a false positive —
corroborated in-page.

**Visual overlays:** injected successfully via Playwright + live-server (started,
then stopped); console reported the findings above; index.html untouched (md5 verified).

## Overall Impression

A disciplined, genuinely specific instrument-toy whose core interaction (drag → snap →
cascade) is legible and delightful — betrayed at its edges by silent failures, an
overloaded Saves panel, and a dead keyboard path. Biggest opportunity: make every
destruction reversible and every refusal speak.

## What's Working

1. The ghost verdict system — 50% ghost, dashed orange/red ring, partner highlight —
   makes the snap promise legible before commit.
2. Instrument restraint is real: one accent spent only on state, tabular mono on every
   live number, hairlines + one shadow — traceable from DESIGN.md to CSS.
3. Edge-state depth: reduced-motion with persisted override, quota toasts, drive-missing
   recovery prompt, corrupt-save quarantine.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Destructive actions without undo or confirmation** — Delete (gear), Delete
   (slot), and Restore destroy work instantly; Restore clobbers the autosave. A toy
   invites experimentation; irreversible loss kills it. Fix: toast-Undo on delete,
   snapshot-before-restore, confirm slot overwrite. → `$impeccable harden`
2. **[P1] Saves panel overload** — six rows × five controls, all equal weight, naming
   model opaque; both assessments flagged the density independently. Fix: occupied slots
   as cards (Restore primary, quiet delete), empty slots collapsed, autosave stated. →
   `$impeccable distill`
3. **[P1] Keyboard users cannot place gears** — tray chips bind pointerdown only;
   Enter/Space on a focused chip is a dead control; no alternative placement path
   (contradicts the approved keyboard a11y bar). Fix: Enter/Space places at canvas
   center; Esc closes Saves/inspector. → `$impeccable adapt`
4. **[P2] Refusals are mute** — the 300ms shake never says why (overlap vs jam) or what
   to do; failed nudges show nothing. Fix: five-word inline reason at the refused ghost;
   shake on failed nudge. → `$impeccable clarify`
5. **[P2] Micro-type and run-state polish** — tray labels at 9.92px fail the 11px floor
   (detector-corroborated); run button shows pressed on an empty bench; paused state
   invisible at a glance. → `$impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

- **Jordan (first-timer):** meshing rule taught only by refusal with no explanation;
  10px tray labels unreadable; "×0.4" is jargon; wrong-gear delete is instant.
- **Sam (accessibility):** focused tray chip does nothing on Enter (dead control);
  canvas lacks role/label; selection/inspector changes not announced (no aria-live);
  ink-2 on panel-deep ~4:1 at small sizes (borderline). Focus rings, names, status
  roles all present and correct.
- **Alex (power):** no undo/Esc/space/duplicate/multi-select; RPM only via slider drag.
